# Modular Furniture Machines - General Guide
General industry knowledge, not personal history. Brand tiers are broad market positioning; exact models, specs and prices vary, so always check with the dealer. There is no single "best" machine: choose by daily volume, panel mix, space, power, operator skill, local service and spare parts, and budget.

## Production flow
Typical panel furniture flow: design and cut list (software) -> optimisation and labelling -> cutting (panel saw, beam saw or CNC nesting) -> edge banding -> boring (multi boring, CNC point-to-point, drilling centre, hinge boring) -> grooving and routing -> pressing or laminating where needed -> sanding and finishing (for painted or veneer work) -> assembly (case clamp, dowel inserter) -> QC -> packing and dispatch.

## Sliding table panel saw (panel saw)
What it does: one operator cuts full boards (plywood, MDF, HDF, particle board, HDHMR) into parts using a sliding table, main blade and scoring blade. Where it fits: first cutting step in small and medium workshops. Features to look for: scoring blade to avoid chipping on laminate, rigid sliding carriage, accurate rip fence and digital readouts, tilting blade for angle cuts, good dust extraction. Brands: premium Altendorf, Felder (Format-4), SCM (Minimax/L'invincibile), Martin; mid Holz-Her, Robland; Indian and Asian value options Jai Industries (J-3200.in), Optimus OptiSaw, Nanxing, KDT and other Chinese makers.

## Beam saw (panel dividing saw)
What it does: a computer controlled saw that clamps and cuts a stack of boards at once along a beam, following an optimised cutting pattern. Where it fits: high volume cutting before edge banding. Features: stack height, pusher and clamps accuracy, optimisation software link, label printer, air floatation tables, rear or front loading. Brands: premium Homag (Holzma), Biesse (Selco), SCM (Gabbiani); mid Holz-Her, KDT, Nanxing; Indian Jai (J-280.in), Optimus OptiCut.
Beam saw vs panel saw: a panel saw is cheaper, flexible and fine for low or mixed volume; a beam saw cuts stacks automatically with higher output, better repeatability and less labour, but costs much more and needs steady volume and optimisation software.

## Edge bander (edge banding machine)
What it does: glues PVC, ABS, acrylic, melamine or veneer edge band to panel edges, then trims, scrapes and buffs them. Where it fits: right after cutting. Units to look for: pre-milling (clean edge before gluing), glue pot (EVA hot melt) or PUR glue for moisture resistance, end trimming, rough and fine trimming, corner rounding, radius and flat scrapers, buffing; feed speed; zero-joint (laser or hot air) on premium machines. Curvilinear or manual edge banders handle curved parts. Brands: premium Homag (EDGETEQ, Ambition), Biesse (Akron, Jade, Stream), SCM (Olimpic), IMA Schelling; mid Holz-Her (Lumina, Streamer), Felder (Format-4), Brandt (Homag group), KDT, Nanxing; Indian and value Jai Industries (J-4550.in and higher), Optimus OptiEdge, Hunny Impex.

## CNC nesting router
What it does: cuts parts of any shape from full boards on a vacuum bed and can also bore and groove in the same operation. Where it fits: replaces or supports the saw for mixed, custom and shaped parts; very common in modular kitchen and wardrobe production. Features: vacuum table and pump capacity, automatic tool changer (ATC), drilling head, labelling and loading/unloading, software (nesting and CAM link to design tools). Brands: premium Biesse (Rover, Skill), Homag, SCM (Morbidelli); mid Holz-Her, KDT, Nanxing; budget Indian and Chinese routers including Jai (J-1530 VT+), Optimus OptiRout.
Best CNC for a small factory: there is no single best. A small factory usually needs a reliable nesting router with ATC, a good vacuum pump, simple software that reads the cut list, and strong local service. Budget Indian or Chinese machines lower entry cost; premium European machines give higher precision, speed and resale but need more investment. Match bed size to board size, check service response time and spare parts, and see a running demo.

## CNC point-to-point machining centre and drilling centres
What it does: machines single panels on pods or suction cups: boring, routing, grooving, edge shaping, hardware pockets. Six-sided drilling machines bore all faces in one pass from a barcode. Features: number of drilling spindles, working sides, barcode or program import, through-feed speed. Brands: premium Biesse (Rover, Brema), Homag (Drillteq, Centateq), SCM (Morbidelli); mid KDT, Nanxing; Indian Optimus OptiBore.

## Multi boring machine
What it does: drills many system holes (32 mm system) for shelf pins, cam locks and dowels in one stroke using several drilling heads. Where it fits: after edge banding in non-CNC lines. Features: number of spindles and heads, vertical and horizontal heads, quick fences and stops. Brands: Biesse, SCM, Homag at the top; KDT, Nanxing; Indian Jai and Optimus multi boring, Hunny Impex.

## Hinge boring machine
What it does: drills the 35 mm concealed hinge cup and fixing holes in shutters. Features: quick depth setting, pattern for Hettich, Hafele, Blum, Ebco hinges, optional hinge insertion. Brands: Blum (Minipress), Hettich, Grass and many local makers.

## Dowel inserter and case clamp
Dowel inserter glues and drives dowels in panels; case clamp presses carcass units square during assembly. Brands: Biesse, Homag, SCM, Koch, Ayen, and local makers.

## Hot press, cold press and laminating
Hot press bonds laminates or veneers onto boards with heat and pressure for faster cycles; cold press uses pressure only and is cheaper but slower. Features: daylight size, platen temperature control, pressure uniformity. Membrane (vacuum membrane) press wraps PVC foil over routed MDF shutters. Brands: Italpresse, Orma, Joos, and Indian makers such as Jai and Hunny Impex.

## Sanding, spray booth and painting line
Wide belt sanders calibrate and finish panels and veneer before painting (SCM, Biesse Viet, Homag Bütfering, Costa; value options from Asian makers). Spray booths and painting lines (water curtain or dry filter booth, drying room, UV line) handle PU, lacquer and duco finishes; key points are airflow, filtration, lighting and drying time. Brands include Cefla and Giardina for lines.

## Labelling and optimisation software
Cut list optimisation reduces board waste and prints part labels with barcodes for edge, boring and assembly. Options include machine-maker software (Biesse, Homag, SCM), and design and cut list tools such as Spazio 3D, MaxCut, Cutlist Optimizer, Mozaik, Polyboard and imos. Choose software that exports directly to your saw and CNC.

## Choosing machines by need and budget
Start-up or small workshop: panel saw, semi-automatic or compact edge bander, multi boring or hinge boring, simple optimisation software. Growing factory: CNC nesting router plus a faster automatic edge bander and a label workflow. Large factory: beam saw, high speed edge bander with PUR or zero-joint, six-sided drilling, and connected software. Always compare service network, spare parts, training, power and space needs, and total running cost, not just purchase price.
